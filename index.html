FAQ

Common questions

How long does a website take?

Most small business sites take two to four weeks, depending on size and how quickly content is ready.

Do I need to provide the text and photos?

It helps, but it's not required. We can help write your content and suggest suitable images.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every site we build is responsive and tested on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Can I update the site myself?

You can, or you can send us changes and we'll make them for you on a maintenance plan.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

Yes. We can set up hosting, connect your domain, and keep everything secure and up to date.
